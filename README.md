## SmartStock: An Inventory and Delivery Tracking System with AI-Assisted Analytics for Glassram Glass and Aluminum Supply

## Main Features

The SmartStock system provides the following core functionalities:

### Automated Inventory Management
Digitalizes the recording and monitoring of glass and aluminum stock levels, ensuring data accuracy and eliminating redundancies associated with manual bookkeeping. The system provides real-time stock visibility and automated low-stock alerts to prevent stockouts and overstocking.

### Integrated Order and Delivery Tracking
Provides real-time status updates for orders (e.g., Processing, In Transit, Delivered) for both staff and customers through a public tracking portal. It enhances logistics efficiency by offering recommended delivery sequences based on location and schedule, improving route and operations efficiency.

### AI-Driven Analytics for Business Intelligence
Analyzes monthly sales and transaction trends to visualize business performance. It identifies top-selling materials on an annual basis, classifies inventory as fast- or slow-moving, and applies predictive modeling using historical sales data to forecast future demand, enabling proactive inventory planning.

## Agile Development Phases

The development process consists of six phases:

1. **Planning Phase** — The overall scope and direction of the system are defined. Existing problems such as manual inventory management and the lack of centralized delivery tracking are analyzed. The system's core modules are identified in collaboration with the store owner to ensure goals align with business needs.

2. **Design Phase** — The system architecture, database structure, user interface, and overall system design are developed based on the identified requirements. Wireframes and system flow diagrams are prepared and validated with the client before development begins.

3. **Development Phase** — System functionalities are incrementally implemented through Agile sprints. Each sprint produces a working version of specific modules (e.g., inventory, tracking, analytics), allowing for continuous refinement based on client feedback.

4. **Testing Phase** — Functional, usability, and performance testing are conducted to evaluate the system's functionality, accuracy, and reliability. Issues identified during testing are corrected to ensure a stable and consistent system.

5. **Deployment Phase** — The completed system is packaged as a desktop application using ElectronJS and deployed for operational use by the Admin/Owner and Staff of Glassram Glass and Aluminum Supply. Users are guided on system usage to ensure a smooth transition from manual processes.

6. **Review Phase** — After deployment, system performance and effectiveness are evaluated based on user feedback from the Admin/Owner and Staff. This phase assesses functionality, usability, and efficiency, providing a basis for future enhancements and continuous improvement.

Throughout the development process, the client (Admin/Owner) actively participates in requirements validation, feature evaluation, and system testing to ensure the final product accurately reflects the operational workflows and needs of Glassram Glass and Aluminum Supply.
