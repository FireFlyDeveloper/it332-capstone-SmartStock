# System Development Methodology

This study adopts the Agile Development Methodology to guide the design and development of the SmartStock system. Agile is selected due to its iterative development approach, flexibility in accommodating evolving system requirements, and emphasis on continuous client feedback and integration.

The development process consists of six phases:

## Planning Phase

The overall scope and direction of the system are defined. Existing problems such as manual inventory management and the lack of centralized delivery tracking are analyzed. The system's core modules are identified in collaboration with the store owner to ensure goals align with business needs.

## Design Phase

The system architecture, database structure, user interface, and overall system design are developed based on the identified requirements. Wireframes and system flow diagrams are prepared and validated with the client before development begins.

## Development Phase

System functionalities are incrementally implemented through Agile sprints. Each sprint produces a working version of specific modules (e.g., inventory, tracking, analytics), allowing for continuous refinement based on client feedback.

## Testing Phase

Functional, usability, and performance testing are conducted to evaluate the system's functionality, accuracy, and reliability. Issues identified during testing are corrected to ensure a stable and consistent system.

## Deployment Phase

The completed system is packaged as a desktop application using ElectronJS and deployed for operational use by the Admin/Owner and Staff of Glassram Glass and Aluminum Supply. Users are guided on system usage to ensure a smooth transition from manual processes.

## Review Phase

After deployment, system performance and effectiveness are evaluated based on user feedback from the Admin/Owner and Staff. This phase assesses functionality, usability, and efficiency, providing a basis for future enhancements and continuous improvement.

## Client Involvement

Throughout the development process, the client (Admin/Owner) actively participates in requirements validation, feature evaluation, and system testing to ensure the final product accurately reflects the operational workflows and needs of Glassram Glass and Aluminum Supply.
